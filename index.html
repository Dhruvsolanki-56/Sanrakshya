How it works

From one visit
to a whole childhood.

The station measures. The app understands. Your paediatrician sees what you see. These are the real app screens, and every reading becomes part of one story that grows with your child.

  1. 01 Measure

    One sitting. Six signs.

    The station captures height, weight, arm circumference, temperature, pulse and oxygen together, and every reading lands in your child’s record. At home, adding one yourself takes seconds.

  2. 02 Understand

    Growth charts, read for you.

    Every reading is plotted against WHO growth standards: percentile, weight, height and BMI. Sanrakshya’s AI reads the trend, not just today’s number, and tells you in plain words how it’s going.

  3. 03 Share

    Your paediatrician sees what you see.

    Your doctor opens the same charts on their side, with every visit, reading and note in one place. Messages, appointments and follow-ups stay in the same thread as the data.

  4. 04 Keep

    Everything, in one passport.

    Their health at a glance. Vaccines on schedule. Meals planned. Milestones remembered. The right specialist, a tap away.

What parents ask first.

What does Sanrakshya measure?

Six signs in one sitting: height, weight, mid-upper arm circumference, temperature, pulse and blood oxygen. At home you can also add readings, vaccinations, meals and milestones yourself in the app.

Does it replace my paediatrician?

No. Sanrakshya keeps the record between visits and reads the trend, so your paediatrician sees the whole picture. Decisions about your child’s care stay with your doctor.

How are the growth charts read?

Every reading is plotted against WHO growth standards, the same references used in paediatric practice. The app looks at the direction of the curve over time, not just one number.

Is it for babies too?

Yes. For babies who can’t stand yet, an infant tray clicks onto the station’s platform, so weight and length are taken lying down and the record starts from the first weeks.

Can I buy the station today?

Not yet. The station shown here is a design concept and the hardware is in development. Leave your details below and we’ll tell you when it’s ready.

Who sees my child’s record?

You, and the paediatricians you choose to share it with. Notes and reports your doctor writes come back to you in the app.
